what is salary managment system?

A salary management system (or payroll management system) is a software or process that automates how an organization calculates, pays, and records employee compensation.

Core Features
• Automated Calculations: Computes gross pay, basic salary, allowances (like HRA or travel), variable bonuses, and overtime.
• Statutory Compliance: Automatically calculates and helps remit government-mandated deductions—such as Provident Fund (EPF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax, and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) in India.
• Attendance & Leave Integration: Links directly with time-tracking tools to adjust pay for unpaid leaves, half-days, or late arrivals.
• Payslip & Document Generation: Generates digital payslips (PDF) and annual tax statements like Form 16.
• Bank Disbursement: Creates bank advice files to transfer net salaries directly into employee bank accounts.
• Employee Self-Service (ESS): Lets staff log in to view payslips, submit tax declarations, apply for leave, and file reimbursement claims.
